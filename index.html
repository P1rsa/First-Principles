Math tutoring that focuses on understanding, not memorizing.

We're Parsa and Max, two Grade 10 IB students who tutor elementary and middle school students in math. We spend more time on why a method works than on drilling the steps, because a student who understands a concept doesn't need to memorize it.

Why our approach

It's possible to get a right answer without understanding anything about why it's right. That kind of knowledge doesn't last, and it doesn't transfer to the next problem. So instead of teaching a list of steps to follow, we teach where those steps come from.

Understand the why
Before we show a shortcut, we make sure a student can see why it works. That way, if they forget the shortcut, they can rebuild it themselves instead of guessing.
Build strong fundamentals
Most struggles in math trace back to a gap a few grades earlier. We're comfortable stepping back to shore up a fundamental before moving forward, even if it's not this year's material.
Adapt to the student's level
Every student explains their thinking differently. We spend the first lesson figuring out how a student actually thinks about a problem, then teach to that, not to a script.
Build confidence through problem-solving
Confidence in math comes from having solved hard problems, not from being told you're good at it. We give students problems slightly past their comfort zone and work through them together.
